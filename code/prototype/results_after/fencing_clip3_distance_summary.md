## Bout summary
This was an active, evenly contested bout across its full three minutes. Fourteen touches were recorded, a rate of roughly 4.7 per minute, so scoring actions came fairly frequently. The fencers spent most of their time at working range: about 45 percent of the recording sat in lunge distance and another 35 percent in advance-lunge, with only occasional excursions to close infighting or fully out of distance. The mean separation in play was about 2.4 m. Both fencers spent more distance advancing than retreating, so neither was clearly on the back foot, though the net displacement figures cannot be trusted here (see caveats).

## Data caveats
These figures come from a computer-vision prototype, so distances are height-normalised estimates rather than precise measurements. The touch count of 14 is human confirmed and reliable, but the touch list records timing only, not who scored, and it does not describe the actions that produced each touch. The scorer_counts here are provided separately and are balanced, but no touch should be attributed from the timestamps themselves. Importantly, net_forward_m and push_share_pct are flagged as unreliable on this recording because uncorrected camera motion has inflated the movement totals, so those figures should not be read as aggression or territorial gain; treat the push and pull comparisons as indicative only.
