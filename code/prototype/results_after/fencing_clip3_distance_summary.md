## Data caveats
These figures come from a computer-vision prototype, so distances are estimates normalised by fencer height rather than precise measurements. All totals cover the whole video, including breaks between touches and walk-backs, because the system does not yet know when touches occur, so they are not in-play-only values. There is no touch or score data here, so nothing above should be read as an account of who scored or of specific actions. Treat these as directional indicators to check against the footage.
