## Data caveats
All distances are computer-vision estimates normalised by fencer height, not precise measurements, and roughly 27 percent of frames used a fallback pose method. The touch list is from an automatic detector, so the count of 14 and the exact timings are approximate and should be confirmed against video; the list also does not say who scored, so no score, winner, or attribution is implied. Critically, the net_forward_m and push_share figures are flagged as unreliable on this recording due to uncorrected camera motion, so nothing here should be read as one fencer being more aggressive or gaining territory. Treat every figure as indicative rather than exact.
