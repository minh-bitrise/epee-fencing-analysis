## Bout summary
This bout was largely fought at longer range. The mean front-foot-to-front-foot distance was about 3.45m, and the pair spent just over half the tracked time (51.3 percent) in the advance-lunge band and a further 32.9 percent out of distance, meaning a step was usually needed before either fencer could score with a lunge. Only 10.2 percent of the time sat in lunge distance, the range where a touch is directly available, and close/infighting was rare at 5.7 percent. Movement volume was fairly balanced between the two, with Fencer 2 covering slightly more ground and edging marginally forward overall (net +0.93m), while Fencer 1 finished marginally net backward (-3.25m). Neither of these net figures is large across a 190 second video, so this reads as a fairly even, distance-managed bout rather than one fencer clearly driving the other.

- Fencer 1's push share (47.0 percent) and net backward figure (-3.25m) suggest a marginally more responsive or retreating pattern, though the gap is small.

## Data caveats
These metrics come from a computer-vision prototype, so distances are height-normalised estimates rather than precise measurements. All totals cover the whole video, including breaks between touches and walk-backs, because the system cannot yet detect when touches occur. There is no touch or score data here, so nothing above should be read as reflecting who won exchanges. Tracking coverage was 73.7 percent with only 11.0 percent from the pose method, so some movement was not captured and the figures should be treated as indicative rather than exact.
