## Data caveats

All distances are computer-vision estimates normalised by fencer height, so treat the bands as approximate. The touch count of 3 is human-confirmed and reliable, but the touch list records only timing, not who scored or how, so no score, winner, or action should be inferred from it. Closing-share figures are measured on unsmoothed positions and are accurate only to a few percentage points, so small gaps between the two fencers are noise. Net forward movement in play is not a displacement and does not mean a fencer finished that far up the piste. There is no measurement of total distance covered, and none should be inferred.
