## Data caveats

These metrics come from a computer-vision prototype, so distances are height-normalised estimates rather than precise measurements. The touch count of 14 is from an automatic detector, not human-confirmed, so treat it as approximate. The touch list records when touches occurred but not who scored them, and scorer_counts is null, so nothing here identifies a winner, a score, or momentum, and none should be inferred. Closing-share figures are window-dependent and accurate only to a few percentage points, so small differences between the fencers are noise. Net forward movement in play is not a displacement and does not mean a fencer finished that far up the piste. No total-distance-covered figure is given because it is not reliably measurable from this data.
